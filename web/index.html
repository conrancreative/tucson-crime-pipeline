About the Tucson bike theft map

This is a live, interactive map of reported bicycle thefts in Tucson, Arizona. Since January 2018, more than 7,200 bike thefts have been reported to the Tucson Police Department and the University of Arizona Police Department — roughly 1,000 stolen bikes every year. Each dot is a reported theft; use the map and table to explore where bikes are stolen, filter by city council ward, and see the most recent reports. The data updates daily.

Frequently asked questions

How many bikes are stolen in Tucson?

More than 7,200 bicycles have been reported stolen in Tucson since 2018 — about 1,000 per year in recent years. The real number is higher, because many bike thefts are never reported to police.

Where are bikes stolen most in Tucson?

The University of Arizona campus and central Tucson see the most bike thefts. Ward 6 records the most reports by far, followed by Ward 3. Neighborhoods around the university — West University, Pie Allen, Sam Hughes and Blenman-Elm — are persistent hotspots.

What should I do if my bike is stolen in Tucson?

File a police report right away — Tucson Police online or by calling 311, or University of Arizona Police if it was taken on campus. Record your bike's serial number, report it stolen on Bike Index, and watch OfferUp, Facebook Marketplace and local pawn shops for it.

How can I avoid getting my bike stolen?

Use a sturdy U-lock rather than a cable lock, and secure both the frame and a wheel to a fixed rack. Park in well-lit, visible spots, avoid leaving a bike locked outside overnight — especially near campus — and register your serial number on Bike Index so a recovered bike can be returned to you.

Where does this data come from?

Reports come from Tucson Police Department open crime data (bicycle larceny) and the University of Arizona Police Department daily activity log, geocoded to map coordinates and refreshed every day. See the Sources tab for the exact feeds and their freshness.
